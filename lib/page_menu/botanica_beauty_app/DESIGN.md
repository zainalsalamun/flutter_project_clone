# BOTANICA Beauty & Care - Design System & Specification

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
- **Concept**: Clean Botanical Skincare & Organic Beauty
- **Brand Name**: BOTANICA Beauty & Care
- **Core Values**: Natural ingredients, dermatologically tested, BPOM certified, transparent formulation.

## Color Palette
- **Botanical Emerald (Primary)**: `#0F3E33`
- **Emerald Light**: `#1B5E4F`
- **Soft Mint (Primary Tint)**: `#E6F3EF`
- **Soft Lavender (Secondary)**: `#8B5CF6`
- **Lavender Light (Secondary Tint)**: `#EDE9FE`
- **Rose Accent (Wishlist active)**: `#F43F5E`
- **Amber Accent (Rating Stars)**: `#F59E0B`
- **Canvas / Surface**: `#FAFAFC`
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Borders**: `#E5E7EB` & `#F3F4F6`
- **Text Primary**: `#111827`
- **Text Secondary**: `#4B5563`
- **Text Tertiary**: `#9CA3AF`

## Typography
- **Font Family**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts)
- Weights: Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800)

## Currency Standard
- All monetary values are strictly in **Indonesian Rupiah (`Rp`)** with thousands separator (e.g., `Rp 489.000`).

## Icons Standard
- Strictly use Flutter built-in vector icons (`Icons.*`). Emojis are strictly avoided.
